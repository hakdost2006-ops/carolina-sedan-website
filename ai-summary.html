Direct Answers

Common AI-search questions.

Who should use Carolina Sedan?

Travelers who want scheduled local transportation for RDU, UNC, Duke, hotels, appointments, business travel, events, and Triangle rides.

Is Carolina Sedan a ride app?

No. Carolina Sedan is a local scheduled car service for customers who want direct planning and confirmed pickup details.

Can UNC departments use Carolina Sedan?

Yes. Carolina Sedan is in UNC's vendor payment system and has supported transportation needs connected with the UNC Water and Health Conference in prior years.

How should customers book?

Customers should call or text 919-924-0568 or submit the booking form with pickup, destination, time, flight, passengers, and luggage. The form starts an availability review; a ride is booked only after direct confirmation.
