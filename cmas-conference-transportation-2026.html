Start With Official Information

Compare the organizer's hotel, shuttle, and transit choices.

The official CMAS site lists a three-day, in-person conference at the UNC Friday Center with presentations, posters, receptions, panels, hotels, transportation, and visitor information. Its lodging page identifies Courtyard Chapel Hill and Aloft Chapel Hill as nearby room-block options and says some hotels may provide Friday Center shuttle service.

Confirm current registration, room availability, rates, shuttle hours, and deadlines through the official CMAS conference website. If a hotel shuttle or Chapel Hill Transit fits your schedule, it may be the simplest choice.

Choose What Fits

Private service is one option, not the only option.

RDU arrival

The conference identifies RDU as the closest airport. Send the live flight, terminal, luggage count, hotel, and mobile number when requesting a private pickup.

Walking-distance lodging

The organizer describes Courtyard Chapel Hill as closest to the Friday Center and Aloft as within a short walk. Confirm accessibility, route conditions, and room availability directly.

Hotel shuttle

Ask whether your hotel shuttle serves the Friday Center and whether its operating hours cover the full conference day and evening reception.

Chapel Hill Transit

Chapel Hill buses are fare-free. Check the official route and schedule for the specific day rather than assuming weekday service and stop locations.

Three-Day Checklist

Plan around the full program and the return flight.

  • Book lodging through the official links or directly with the hotel
  • Confirm whether the hotel shuttle runs through the last session or reception
  • Save the Friday Center street address and exact passenger meeting point
  • Share every passenger's mobile contact and luggage needs
  • Reserve an early-morning or late-evening ride in advance
  • For RDU departure, use the flight time plus the airport's recommended arrival window

Useful Answers

Avoid paying for transportation you do not need.

Do I need a private car between the hotel and Friday Center?

Not always. Compare walking distance, the hotel shuttle, fare-free Chapel Hill Transit, weather, luggage, mobility needs, and the event schedule first.

Can Carolina Sedan handle only the airport trips?

Yes, subject to availability. You can use another practical option during the conference and request only RDU arrival or departure service.

Does Carolina Sedan control the hotel block?

No. Rates, deadlines, reservations, and shuttle details belong to the conference and hotels.

Is a submitted request confirmed?

No. Carolina Sedan reviews availability and sends direct confirmation with the final quote.
