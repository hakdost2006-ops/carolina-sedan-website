Corporate FAQs

Straightforward answers for planners and travelers.

Can I request RDU-to-RTP transportation?

Yes. Send the flight, traveler, luggage, destination, and mobile contact details. Carolina Sedan will review availability and confirm the pickup plan and final quote.

Can one request include several stops?

Yes. Submit the complete itinerary for review. Carolina Sedan will confirm what can be accommodated and how the transportation should be scheduled.

Can an assistant or department arrange the ride?

Yes. Include both the traveler contact and the person responsible for itinerary or billing questions.

Is submitting the form the same as booking?

No. The form starts an availability review. The reservation is booked only after Carolina Sedan confirms it directly.
