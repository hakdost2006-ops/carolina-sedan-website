What Is Confirmed

The date and venue are public; more attendee details may follow.

The Duke Center for Antimicrobial Stewardship and Infection Prevention lists the DICON-DASON Fall Educational Symposium as an in-person event on November 20 at the Friday Conference Center at UNC-Chapel Hill.

The current save-the-date does not provide a complete attendee agenda or official lodging plan. Recheck the official symposium page before making nonrefundable travel arrangements.

Practical Planning

Choose lodging and flights around the actual program.

RDU arrival

For a morning program, arriving the previous day may reduce risk. Wait for the final start time before purchasing travel solely on that assumption.

Hotel area

Friday Center-area lodging can simplify venue access. Downtown Chapel Hill or Durham may fit other meetings better. Compare the whole itinerary.

Same-day departure

Do not schedule a tight flight until the final session end time is known. Include time for walking, luggage retrieval, road travel, and airport processing.

Small-group request

Provide every passenger's pickup, mobile number, luggage, and flight. Capacity is reviewed before transportation is confirmed.

Before You Book

Turn the save-the-date into a complete travel plan.

  • Confirm registration, start time, end time, and attendee instructions
  • Confirm the Friday Center entrance and pickup point
  • Book lodging directly and review its cancellation terms
  • Share airline, flight number, luggage, and traveler mobile number
  • Keep organizer updates separate from transportation confirmation
  • Use RDU's two-hour domestic or three-hour international arrival guidance

Useful Answers

What attendees should know.

Can I request a ride before the agenda is final?

Yes, but mark uncertain times clearly and update the request when the organizer publishes the program.

Can Carolina Sedan recommend a hotel block?

No official block is stated on the current event page. Compare hotel location and terms yourself, then send the confirmed address.

Is this medical transportation?

No. Carolina Sedan offers ordinary scheduled passenger transportation, not clinical or emergency transport.

When is the reservation confirmed?

After availability, schedule, capacity, and final quote review.
