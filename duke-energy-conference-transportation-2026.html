What Is Confirmed

The date and conference scale are public; the final travel details are not.

Duke's Office of Climate and Sustainability lists the 18th annual Duke University Energy Conference for November 11. The announcement says the event draws more than 400 attendees and includes keynote speakers, panels, networking, and industry experts as part of Energy Week at Duke.

The current announcement does not provide the final conference venue or complete program. Check the official Duke announcement and its linked event site before confirming the campus drop-off.

Before Travel

Recheck details instead of relying on an early save-the-date.

  • Confirm registration and the final event venue
  • Save the exact building entrance and organizer check-in time
  • Send airline, flight number, hotel, and passenger mobile number
  • Separate speaker, attendee, dinner, and return-flight movements
  • Update the reservation if the organizer changes the program
  • Use RDU's two-hour domestic or three-hour international arrival guidance

Useful Answers

What can be planned now.

Can I request transportation before the venue is announced?

Yes. Provide the confirmed flight and hotel details, state that the venue is pending, and update the request when the organizer publishes it.

Can Carolina Sedan transport a conference-sized group?

Carolina Sedan is designed for private and small-group scheduled rides. Submit the passenger and luggage count for an honest capacity review.

Is Carolina Sedan the official conference provider?

No. It is an independent local transportation option.

Does a request guarantee service?

No. Service is confirmed only after availability, timing, capacity, and quote review.
