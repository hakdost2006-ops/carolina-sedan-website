Family Weekend FAQs

Plan before the busiest parts of the weekend.

Can I request more than one ride?

Yes. Submit the full weekend itinerary. Carolina Sedan will confirm what can be accommodated, pickup details, and the final quote.

Do you serve Duke hotels and both campuses?

Carolina Sedan accepts requests for RDU, Durham hotels, Duke destinations, Chapel Hill, and nearby Triangle routes, subject to availability.

Does a parking pass include Carolina Sedan?

No. Duke's registration, parking, and ticketing are separate from Carolina Sedan's independent transportation service.

When is the reservation final?

A website submission is a request. The ride is booked only after Carolina Sedan confirms availability, timing, and the quote directly.
