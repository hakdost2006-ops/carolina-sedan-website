Start With Official Information

Confirm the program before finalizing the ride plan.

Duke's official conference page lists the symposium at the Karsh Alumni and Visitors Center, 2080 Duke University Road, and recommends that air travelers arrive through RDU by the afternoon of October 9. It also lists JB Duke Hotel, Washington Duke Inn, and AC Hotel Durham as lodging options.

Review registration, agenda, travel, and any schedule changes on the official Duke symposium page. Carolina Sedan does not manage the conference, registration, lodging, or program.

Arrival And Hotels

Make the first day easier before the flight leaves.

Send the live flight details

Provide the airline, flight number, scheduled arrival, passenger mobile number, and luggage count. Update the driver if the itinerary changes before departure.

Choose lodging around the full itinerary

A Duke-area hotel may simplify an early venue arrival. If downtown Durham dinners or meetings matter more, compare that location before booking.

Keep hotel reservations separate

Confirm room availability and cancellation terms directly with the hotel. A transportation request does not reserve a room or conference registration.

Use one itinerary for several guests

For speakers or a small group, list each flight, hotel, mobile contact, and pickup time so the movements can be reviewed together.

Event-Day Advice

Plan the venue entrance, not only the street address.

  • Confirm the event entrance and check-in time with the organizer
  • Ask the hotel where the vehicle may safely meet you
  • Allow extra time for registration, walking, and an unfamiliar campus
  • Keep the traveler's mobile phone on at pickup time
  • For the return, use the actual flight time and required airport arrival time
  • RDU advises arriving two hours before domestic and three hours before international departures

Useful Answers

Before requesting a car.

Should I book the RDU arrival and departure together?

Usually, yes. Sharing both flights and the hotel makes it easier to review the complete schedule and avoid a last-minute return arrangement.

Can Carolina Sedan reserve my hotel?

No. Hotel and conference reservations must be made directly with the businesses and organizers listed on the official event page.

Is the request instantly confirmed?

No. The form begins an availability and quote review. A ride is booked only after Carolina Sedan confirms it directly.

Is Carolina Sedan part of the symposium?

No. Carolina Sedan is an independent local transportation provider.
