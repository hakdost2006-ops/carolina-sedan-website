Workshop Travel Planning

Plan the airport, hotel, campus, and return trip together.

The official workshop site lists the 2026 Duke-ORNL Workshop on Advanced Microscopy and Data-Driven Materials Science for October 12-13 at Schiciano Auditorium in Duke University's Fitzpatrick Center in Durham. It identifies RDU as the closest airport. Confirm registration, parking, accommodations, and program details on the official workshop website.

Carolina Sedan independently accepts transportation requests from workshop attendees, speakers, researchers, and guests. Submit the full itinerary so airport arrivals, hotel transfers, campus drop-offs, dinners, and departures can be reviewed as one schedule.

Workshop Transportation FAQs

What attendees and planners should know.

Can I request an RDU airport pickup?

Yes. Include the flight, arrival time, hotel or destination, passenger count, luggage, and mobile contact for availability review.

Can one request include several rides?

Yes. Submit the full itinerary. Carolina Sedan will confirm which rides can be accommodated and provide the final quote.

Is Carolina Sedan affiliated with the workshop?

No. Carolina Sedan is an independent transportation provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official transportation partner of the workshop, Duke University, or Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

When is transportation confirmed?

The website form starts an availability review. A ride is booked only after Carolina Sedan directly confirms the schedule and final quote.
