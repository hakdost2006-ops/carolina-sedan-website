Durham Alert: Duke Street Closure (Trinity Ave-Monmouth Ave)

Starting in late May 2026, a Duke Street closure tied to South Ellerbe Restoration work may affect trips to Duke, downtown Durham, medical appointments, and RDU.

Route Planning

What it means for airport and appointment rides.

If you travel through downtown Durham, especially between Trinity Park, Old North Durham, and the Duke Street corridor, plan for a major route change starting in late May 2026.

The City of Durham says Duke Street will be closed between Trinity Avenue and Monmouth Avenue as part of work connected to the South Ellerbe Restoration Project. Drivers should watch for message boards and follow detour signage, with traffic directed to Washington Street or Buchanan Boulevard to get around the work area.

Why this matters for Carolina Sedan customers

Many Carolina Sedan trips rely on predictable timing, especially RDU airport transfers, Duke Health and nearby medical appointments, downtown Durham dining and hotel rides, university travel, and business trips where on-time arrival is non-negotiable.

With the closure in place, normal straight-through routes can turn into detours, especially during peak traffic times. RDU also recently reported two of its top 10 busiest days on record this month, so airport travelers should be extra careful with pickup timing.

Practical tips to avoid delays

Add buffer time for trips that typically use Duke Street between Trinity and downtown. Share your exact destination entrance, such as a main lobby or side entrance, so your driver can choose the cleanest approach. For RDU rides, consider an earlier pickup if your route might cross central Durham during a busy travel period.
