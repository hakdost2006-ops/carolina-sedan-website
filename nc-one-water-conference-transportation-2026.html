Attendee Checklist

Avoid conflicts between checkout, sessions, and flights.

  • Confirm the latest program and your required sessions
  • Check hotel-block deadlines and book directly with the hotel
  • Ask whether the hotel can store luggage after checkout
  • Choose a precise downtown meeting point away from blocked curbs
  • Allow time for the final session, luggage retrieval, and RDU travel
  • RDU recommends arrival two hours before domestic and three hours before international departures

Useful Answers

Keep the plan simple.

Do I need daily car service?

Not if your hotel, conference, and meals are comfortably walkable. Many attendees may need only RDU arrival and departure rides.

Can a team share one reservation request?

Yes. Include every traveler's flight, luggage, hotel, mobile contact, and timing so vehicle capacity and sequencing can be reviewed.

Does Carolina Sedan reserve conference rooms?

No. Registration and hotel reservations remain entirely separate from transportation.

Is the ride confirmed when submitted?

No. Submission begins an availability and quote review; direct confirmation is required.
