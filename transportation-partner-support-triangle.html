Three steps from request to local execution.

  2. Confirm the operating planWe review coverage, vehicle capacity, communication expectations, quote, and billing contact before acceptance.
  3. Keep one line of communicationYour designated coordinator receives the confirmed pickup plan and can relay material itinerary changes.

Request Checklist

What helps us answer quickly and accurately.

  • Company, planner, or organization name
  • Primary operations and after-hours contact
  • Passenger names and working mobile numbers
  • Flights, terminals, hotels, entrances, and every stop
  • Passenger and luggage count for each movement
  • Vehicle preference and any accessibility requirement
  • Meet-and-greet or signage instructions
  • Quote, billing, confirmation, and update requirements

Partner FAQs

Clear expectations before an assignment is accepted.

Who can request partner support?

Transportation operators, travel agencies, DMCs, corporate travel planners, university teams, conference organizers, and delegation coordinators can request local coverage.

Can you handle a multi-day delegation schedule?

Submit the entire schedule for review. Carolina Sedan will confirm which movements and vehicle needs can be supported.

Do you provide motorcoaches or mass shuttles?

Carolina Sedan focuses on private and small-group service. Larger requirements must be reviewed and are never represented as confirmed unless suitable capacity is specifically arranged.

Is a request automatically accepted?

No. Service begins only after Carolina Sedan confirms availability, operating details, capacity, and pricing directly.
