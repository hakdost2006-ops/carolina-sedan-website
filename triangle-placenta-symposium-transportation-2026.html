Start With Official Information

Plan around the published 8:30 a.m. start.

Duke's Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology lists the second Triangle Placenta Symposium for December 10 from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. in the Trent Semans Center Great Hall. The in-person gathering includes labs and participants from Duke, UNC-Chapel Hill, NC State, NIEHS, and regional research and industry partners.

The official page also lists an October 16 abstract deadline. Confirm registration, abstract instructions, and any program changes through the official Duke symposium page.

Morning And Hotel Planning

Remove avoidable pressure from an early start.

Arrive the day before

For nonlocal travelers, a December 9 arrival may be more practical than relying on a same-morning flight. Confirm lodging and weather policies directly.

Choose a Durham hotel

Duke-area lodging can shorten the morning trip. Downtown Durham may fit evening plans. Compare location, cancellation terms, and the rest of your itinerary.

Confirm the entrance

Use Trent Semans Center Great Hall as the destination, then confirm the event entrance and registration time with the organizer.

Coordinate several institutions

For a small group, provide each pickup address, passenger contact, luggage count, and required arrival time rather than one broad instruction.

Attendee Checklist

Build enough margin into a December travel day.

  • Confirm registration and abstract requirements with the event
  • Choose lodging after comparing the morning venue trip
  • Allow time for check-in before the 8:30 a.m. program
  • Share exact passenger contacts and pickup addresses
  • Monitor weather and flight status before traveling
  • For RDU departure, plan for the 5 p.m. end plus road and airport processing time

Useful Answers

Before finalizing the trip.

Is a same-day flight into RDU advisable?

An early program makes that a higher-risk choice. Compare flight reliability, baggage time, RDU-to-Durham travel, and event check-in before deciding.

Can Carolina Sedan transport presenters from different hotels?

Possibly, subject to time and capacity. Submit each pickup and required arrival time so the route can be reviewed honestly.

Is this clinical transportation?

No. Carolina Sedan provides ordinary scheduled passenger transportation and does not provide medical or emergency transport.
