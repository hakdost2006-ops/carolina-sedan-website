Smart planning, smooth travel

Local rides, airport trips, appointments, and events need a little extra buffer this week.

Carolina Sedan's recent Google and Facebook travel advisory highlighted a simple but important point: local rides are smoother when travelers plan ahead instead of depending on last-minute availability.

Summer travel remains strong across the Triangle. That can affect airport traffic, RDU parking, security timing, hotel pickups, campus visits, and evening plans. If your schedule includes a flight, appointment, dinner reservation, meeting, or event, give yourself more time than the map suggests.

High heat is also part of the week. Stay hydrated, plan comfortable pickup points when possible, and never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle.

Afternoon storms can move quickly through central North Carolina. Scattered storms may slow traffic on I-40, I-85, NC-54, downtown routes, and roads leading to RDU. Even a short storm can make curbside pickups, airport arrivals, and appointment trips take longer.
