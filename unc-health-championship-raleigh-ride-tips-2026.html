Tournament Weekend Planning

Plan the ride before the day gets busy.

If you are traveling around the Triangle this weekend for the UNC Health Championship at Raleigh Country Club, Thursday, May 28 through Sunday, May 31, 2026, a little transportation planning can save a lot of stress.

The tournament can bring busy arrival windows, full schedules, and visitors moving between Raleigh, Chapel Hill, Carrboro, Durham, hotels, restaurants, and Raleigh-Durham International Airport. Whether you are staying in Raleigh, coming in from Chapel Hill/Carrboro, or meeting friends from Durham, the goal is the same: arrive on time without circling for parking or watching the clock in traffic.

1. Build in extra time, especially during afternoon peaks.

Even when the drive looks short on a map, event traffic, work zones, and last-minute detours can add minutes you did not budget for. If you have a tee time to catch, a dinner reservation, or an RDU departure window, leaving earlier is the easiest upgrade you can make.

2. Plan your last mile as carefully as the main drive.

Big event weekends often mean temporary routing changes or closures in parts of Raleigh. If your plans include downtown before or after the tournament, check Raleigh's published closures and detours list so you are not surprised by a closed block when you are already running late.

3. Weather can change timing, too.

Late-May weather in central North Carolina can bring pop-up heavy rain that slows traffic and makes curbside drop-offs take longer. Even isolated pockets of heavier rainfall can create localized backups, so give yourself a little room in the schedule.

4. If you are flying in or out of RDU, keep airport timing simple.

If your tournament weekend includes air travel, remember that airport construction and busy periods can make the experience feel slower than usual. RDU recommends arriving at least two hours before domestic flights and three hours before international flights.
