Homecoming FAQs

Clear expectations before confirmation.

Can I book the entire weekend at once?

You can submit the full itinerary for review. Carolina Sedan will confirm which movements can be accommodated and quote them directly.

Do you provide RDU transportation?

Yes. Include flight information, hotel or destination, passengers, luggage, and a working mobile contact.

Can you promise a stadium entrance?

No. Traffic controls can change. Use the location and timing confirmed for your specific reservation.

Is this an official UNC service?

No. Carolina Sedan is an independent local transportation provider. Event registration, parking, and tickets remain with UNC.
