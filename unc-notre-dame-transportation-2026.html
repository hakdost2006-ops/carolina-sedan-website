Game-Weekend FAQs

Direct answers for visitors.

Can I request transportation from RDU?

Yes. Send the flight, destination, passengers, luggage, and contact details. Carolina Sedan will review availability and confirm the pickup plan and final quote.

Can you guarantee a stadium pickup point?

No pickup location is guaranteed before current traffic restrictions are reviewed. Follow the pickup instructions Carolina Sedan confirms for your reservation.

Should I wait for the kickoff time?

You can request availability now and provide the announced time later. Final transportation timing must be confirmed directly.

Does the website request confirm my ride?

No. Submitting the form starts an availability review. The ride is booked only after Carolina Sedan confirms it.
