Start With Official Information

Get the exact daily meeting point from the organizer.

The official Visual Discovery Conference website describes an October 12-16 gathering at UNC-Chapel Hill for students and teachers from participating design schools around the world. Workshops, talks, an exhibition, a competition, networking, and a final presentation are part of the week.

Because a campus-wide location is not the same as a building entrance, attendees should confirm each day's venue in the official timetable or registration communication. Check the official conference website before setting final pickup and drop-off locations.

A Better Week-Long Plan

Organize changing locations without overbooking transportation.

Arrival day

Send the airline, flight number, luggage count, and Chapel Hill hotel address. International travelers should also allow for immigration, baggage, and possible connection delays.

Hotel choice

Choose lodging after comparing the confirmed campus meeting points. Downtown Chapel Hill can be useful for Franklin Street; another area may be easier for the actual program.

Daily campus movement

Use walking or Chapel Hill Transit when it fits. Reserve a car for airport movements, luggage, unfamiliar off-campus destinations, guests, or fixed-time commitments.

Group coordination

Do not assume every participant has the same arrival. Group requests should list each traveler's flight, mobile contact, luggage, hotel, and schedule.

Attendee Checklist

Information to confirm before the conference week.

  • Registration eligibility and the latest official timetable
  • Exact building and entrance for each scheduled session
  • Hotel address and its check-in and luggage-storage policies
  • Arrival flight, terminal, passenger count, and luggage count
  • Evening events or off-campus activities requiring a separate ride
  • Return flight timing; RDU recommends two hours early domestically and three internationally

Useful Answers

Plan transportation around the program.

Should I reserve a car for every day?

Not necessarily. First review the timetable, hotel location, walking distance, and transit options. Prearranged service is most useful for airports, luggage, fixed appointments, and off-campus plans.

Can several international arrivals be combined?

Sometimes, if timing, passenger capacity, and luggage permit. Submit every flight separately so Carolina Sedan can review the practical options.

Does the ride request register me for VDC?

No. Registration and conference participation are handled only by the event organizers.

When is a ride confirmed?

Only after Carolina Sedan reviews availability, timing, passenger needs, and the final quote.
