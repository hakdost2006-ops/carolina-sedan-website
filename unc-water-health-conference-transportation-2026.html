Conference FAQs

What attendees and planners should know.

Does Carolina Sedan provide RDU transportation?

Yes. Attendees can request scheduled RDU arrivals and departures. Include the flight, hotel or destination, passenger count, luggage, and mobile contact.

Can I submit a multi-day itinerary?

Yes. Send every requested movement for review. Carolina Sedan will confirm what can be accommodated, the pickup plan, and the final quote.

Is Carolina Sedan the official conference provider?

No. Carolina Sedan is an independent transportation provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official transportation partner of the conference or UNC.

When is the ride confirmed?

The website form begins an availability review. The ride is booked only after Carolina Sedan sends direct confirmation.

Independent provider notice

Carolina Sedan Service is an independent transportation provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official transportation partner of the UNC Water and Health Conference or the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Event details may change; confirm the current schedule with the organizer.
